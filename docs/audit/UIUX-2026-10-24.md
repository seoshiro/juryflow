## Minimalist design decisions

| Significant graphic | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Bar logo and favicon | Keep the crisp vector identity. |
| Small event mark/star | Keep as a restrained event anchor. |
| Empty-grid glyph | Keep; reduce its excessive surrounding empty space. |
| Schedule grid and mobile agenda | Keep as the primary information artifact; wrap long labels. |
| Judge initials | Keep identification without portraits or external images. |
| Availability bars/ticks | Keep with readable numeric counts and the existing detailed slot picker. |
| Monochrome action icons | Keep their consistent weight; enlarge useful mobile hit areas and retain visible labels. |

No photograph, raster illustration, AI image, font download or new dependency was added. Hierarchy, spacing, navigation and text legibility provide the improvement.
